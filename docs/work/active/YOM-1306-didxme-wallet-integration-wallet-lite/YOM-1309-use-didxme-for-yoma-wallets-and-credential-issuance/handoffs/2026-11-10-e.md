## Recovery Contract

- `SSI.CredentialIssuance.CredentialId` is the native holder-wallet credential ID, not the
  issuance journal ID or issuer-side credential ID.
- `_Referent_Client` carries the stable domain issuance schedule ID. Recovery finds that
  reference in the holder wallet and restores the actual credential ID, schema version and date.
- An issuance journal coordinates new issuance, delivery and webhook correlation. A missing
  journal does not prevent recovery of an accepted credential. It is not recreated with guessed
  issuer/session IDs; that attempt's issuer/session audit history remains unavailable.
- Preset mappings and the participant's identity/tenant mapping remain necessary. This test
  does not represent recovery after losing all provider tables.
- Existing Deleted journal entries from Replay may remain. Zero active rows is intentional
  after missing-journal recovery. Wallet reads and Replay do not require a replacement journal.
- Missing wallet data after a recorded delivery attempt stays an uncertain outcome requiring
  reconciliation; this test does not authorize blind redelivery or make the systems atomic.
