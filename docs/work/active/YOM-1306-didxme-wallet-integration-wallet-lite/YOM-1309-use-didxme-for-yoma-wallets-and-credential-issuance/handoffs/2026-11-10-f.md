## Gotchas

- The transition tests simulate a stale read at the lock boundary; they do not prove deployed multi-instance behavior.
- Background login suppression assumes the configured assertion client is dedicated to that integration. Ordinary youth clients must remain separate.
- Client credentials generate CLIENT_LOGIN events, which the current Compose/Helm webhook allowlist does not forward. The controller also handles only LOGIN, REGISTER and UPDATE_PROFILE. A general B2B/M2M classifier is unnecessary here and could suppress legitimate user flows on mixed-use clients. No Keycloak or Helm configuration was changed.
- Recovery is not an atomic transaction across systems. An uncertain delivery with no accepted match remains subject to reconciliation.
