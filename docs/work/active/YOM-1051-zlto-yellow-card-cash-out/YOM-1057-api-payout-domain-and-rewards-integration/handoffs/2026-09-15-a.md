## Template Contract

Use four Yoma-branded dynamic templates, following the verification notification pattern.
Each template defines its own subject, heading and body. Append {{subjectSuffix}} to its subject
(supplied by the provider outside Production). Greeting: {{recipientDisplayName}}.
Shared payload fields:

- amount / amountFormatted: payout amount, formatted to two invariant decimal places.
- currency: payout currency (currently USD).
- zltoAmount / zltoAmountFormatted: actual linked reward amount, formatted as whole ZLTO.
- yoIDWalletURL: stable Yoma /yoid/wallet link. Button text: View My Wallet.

Use normal escaped template expressions. No Yoma/IXO references, raw status, provider errors, KYC/bank details, current wallet balances,
temporary hosted URLs or recalculated historical exchange rate. The wallet link does not claim
that /payout/latest still refers to this email's payout.

## Gotchas

- Best-effort is intentional, not exactly-once delivery. A crash after commit or email failure may
  lose the email; subsequent terminal events do not retry it. No backlog or historical email replay.
- Early failures before a recorded reward settlement do not emit these settlement emails. An
  ambiguous reservation expiry threshold alone is not proof that ZLTO has been released.
- Completed wording says cash-out completed / ZLTO deducted, not that funds reached a bank account.
  IXO's separate post-Completed delivery-leg retries do not trigger another Yoma outcome.
- Missing/incorrect deployed template IDs or inactive templates may cause logged email failures;
  payout settlement is unaffected.
- Existing preference-filter failure behavior is preserved, as are SendGrid environment gates and sender.
