## Gotchas

- IXO accepts the Yoma payout id as both idempotency key and reconciliation lookup reference.
- IXO returns both initiated and processing; both are active and normalize to Yoma Processing.
- A hosted session lasts approximately 30 minutes, but the payout remains resumable for 24 hours. Once
  confirmed, it cannot expire and reaches Completed or Failed within approximately six hours at worst.
- Webhooks and reconciliation remain the authoritative early commit/release triggers. The 30-hour ZLTO
  reservation is only the final safety net.
- Transport timeouts, hosted-session expiry and ZLTO reservation expiry are separate controls. Do not
  derive the reservation duration from the 60-second IXO HTTP timeout.
- Do not place the client secret in tracked appsettings or this documentation.
