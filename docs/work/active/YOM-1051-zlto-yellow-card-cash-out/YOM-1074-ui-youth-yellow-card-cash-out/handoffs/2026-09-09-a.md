**Three design decisions worth knowing about**, all recorded in `feature.md` and all
browser-verified: the ledger now shows **only rows that carry information** (a movement brings its

`tabular-nums`. The first of those **supersedes "five rows in fixed order" in the design brief** —
please read that decision before redrawing Flow A.

### Marketplace layout

- `components/PageBackground.tsx` — **gained a wrapper mode.** Supplying `children` puts the band
  in normal flow so its height is its content's height. Without `children` it behaves exactly as
  before, so the 21 other callers are untouched.
- The Marketplace hero now uses that: **the blue band _is_ the hero section.** It was an absolute
  `h-80` behind the page, which meant 320px of blue and the hero's real height were two independent
  numbers — unworkable here, since the ledger grows and shrinks with the wallet's state. The
  results grid was straddling the boundary. Now the band ends where the hero ends and
  "Filter by"/results are on the page background. `pt-20` sits _inside_ the band so the blue still
  runs behind the fixed navbar.
- The hero is **one box with a reserved `min-h-[168px]`, both states centred inside it**. The
  logged-out and logged-in branches have different natural heights and swap when the profile atom
  hydrates, which shifted the whole page down on every load.
- Stacked padding removed: the container's `py-4` top and the content wrapper's `mt-2 p-4` →
  a single `py-6` between band and results.

- `components/Payout/CashOutGate.tsx` — **new.** Seven blocking states with their copy in one

- `api/services/payout.ts` — **new.** `listPayoutCountries()`; **503 resolves to `null`**, not an

- `api/models/payout.ts`, `api/models/user.ts` — `UserProfilePayout` corrected from the stale

## Gotchas

- **Two live bugs were fixed in passing; both were latent until `08cb6c10a` reached web, and both
  turned a null into a `0`.**
  - `WalletCard`'s empty state was `(zlto?.total ?? 0) === 0 && pendingPayout === 0`. With `total`
    nullable, an offline wallet collapsed to "You will receive ZLTO for completing opportunities" —
    a youth mid-payout told their earnings never happened. It now requires a _known_ zero.
  - `marketplace/[country]/index.tsx` did `userProfile.zlto.available < item.amount`. `null < n` is
    `true`, so an offline wallet would report "Insufficient funds" against a real balance, and the
    analytics `shortfall` would have been computed against 0.
  - Both are the trap the feature doc warns about: _`null + 5` is `5` and `0` is a legitimate
    balance_. These two were what `tsc` caught once the old field was deleted — which is why it
    gets deleted.
- **`UserProfilePayout` was stale in the same way**, and the gate would have been its first reader.
  Nothing read it before, so nothing was broken. Fourth occurrence of this class of break.
- The ledger **always reconciles on screen**, because the server derives `balance` from `available`.
  Nobody should add a "figures don't add up" check off the back of it.
- **The compact variant uses no opacity on purpose.** Measured: white on `--color-blue` `#4cade9`
  is **2.48:1**, which fails AA at any size. That is pre-existing across the whole hero band, not
  introduced here, but it means tinting secondary rows to make them recede would push a failing
  ratio further down. Raised as a brand decision in `feature.md`, not fixed.
- `min-h-[168px]` on the hero removes the shift for every state **except a cash-out in flight**,
  which adds two ledger rows (~203px measured). Reserving for that would have put 35px of dead
  space in the common case and pushed the hero out of the 320px blue band. Deliberate trade.
- `WalletCard` passes the ledger `flex-1 justify-center` so a one-row wallet sits centred in the
  fixed-height dashboard card rather than pinned to the top with an inch of white beneath it.
