## Gotchas

- **The payout status enum exists; the youth-facing contract does not carry it.** Seven statuses
  (`Initiated`, `Processing`, **`ReconciliationRequired`** — the one the plan did not have —
  `Completed`, `Failed`, `Cancelled`, `Expired`), with the first three being "active". The profile
  exposes one boolean derived from an amount. So:
  - the resume panel deliberately shows **no status line** (C1 vs C2 is not decidable), and
  - **do not add a completed/failed screen by inference.** A commit and a release both leave
    `pendingPayout` at 0, so the wallet cannot tell you which happened, and guessing is guessing
    about someone's money.
- **`payout.amount` is USD; `zlto.pendingPayout` is the ZLTO.** Two figures for one payout, in two
  places, in two units, both called "amount". The resume panel shows both, labelled.
- **The rate line is inferred, not fetched.** Dividing the 2dp estimate naively gives 45.0045 at a
  45 rate — a rate that visibly wobbles while a money amount is being typed. `conversion.ts` shows
  it only at a precision the rounding interval supports and hides it below about a dollar. If the
  rate lands on `ConversionResponse`, delete the module.
- **Two amount rules come back as 500s, not 400s.** `ArgumentOutOfRangeException` (≤ 0) and
  `ArgumentException` (fractional) are unmapped in `ExceptionResponseMiddleware`. They are guarded
  client-side; do not remove those guards on the grounds that "the server validates it".
- **"There are insufficient funds available to complete this payout" is the *paused* state, not a
  field error** — it is Treasury capacity, checked again under the lock, i.e. the preview's
  `treasuryFundsAvailable: false` arriving a moment later. The only field error in the flow is
  `"Insufficient reward balance for payout…"`, and it is shown **without a figure**: it means the
  ledger was stale, so quoting its number would contradict the refusal.
- **There is no provider redirect back into Yoma.** Nothing in `PayoutRequest` carries a return URL,
  so the hosted journey opens in a new tab and "returning" is this tab regaining visibility. The
  hand-off screen doubles as the blocked-popup fallback because the round trip puts `window.open`
  outside the youth's tap and the browser will not say which happened — and the Zlto is already
  reserved by then, so it must never read as "nothing happened".
- **`CustomModal`'s box is `fixed inset-0`**, so a dialog is as tall as its `max-h` whatever the
  content until you add `md:h-fit`. That was the design review's dead-space note, and it will bite
  the next dialog too.
- **daisyUI's `btn-disabled` is unreadable on the hero band** — pale grey on `--color-blue`. The
  compact button uses a translucent white pill with `text-gray-dark` instead; on white, daisyUI's
  look is the one the board draws.
- The dev server needs `mise exec -- pnpm dev`: the default `node` on PATH is 20 and corepack's
  pnpm fails on it with `ERR_UNKNOWN_BUILTIN_MODULE`.

## Open Questions

1. **Payout status on `UserProfilePayout`.** Even just "resumable / processing" would let Flow C say
   which it is. The full status would let Flow D exist.
2. **A youth-readable terminal outcome.** Anything that answers "did my last cash out complete?"

   "being processed" and then never told anything.

   money surface.

drafts, now in one file. Two knowingly diverge from the boards: the result body adds "if you haven't
already", because the youth may reach that screen having abandoned the hosted journey rather than
finished it, and Yoma cannot tell the difference; and the resume panel has no "Waiting for you to
finish" pill, per the gotcha above.
