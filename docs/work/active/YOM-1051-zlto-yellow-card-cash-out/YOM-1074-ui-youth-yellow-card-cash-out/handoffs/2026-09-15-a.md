### Buttons

Secondary actions are **full-width outlined pills**. `w-full` on a ghost button fills the row
invisibly — identical at 80px and 480px — so Cancel, Back, Close and "Back to my wallet" carry a
border on white: the primary's footprint, less weight. The gate's centred row of two auto-width
buttons stacks the same way; it was the last screen not following the pattern and wrapped awkwardly
at 320px.

heading, icon, tone and verb lived in `CashOutEntry`, so every caller had to repeat it — and the dev
gallery got it wrong twice. Derived inside the component now.

## Gotchas

- **⚠️ A narrow headless window does not test a narrow layout.** Chrome will not open below ~500px
  on Windows, so `--window-size=320` lays out at 500 and crops the screenshot. This produced a false
  "the whole product overflows on mobile" finding on 2026-09-11, since retracted. Use the gallery's
  width preview (an iframe, so a real 320px viewport) or devtools emulation.
- **`w-full` on a ghost button is invisible.** If a secondary is meant to read as full width, it
  needs a border or a fill — otherwise it looks unchanged and the next person "fixes" it again.
- **The gallery must derive what the flow derives.** `describeOutcome(...).resolved` and the resume
  panel's `state` are the two current cases; hardcoding either has twice made the gallery show a
  screen the flow renders correctly. Pass every handler the flow passes, too.
- Yoma's consent banner covers the lower part of the preview until dismissed once — real app chrome,
  not gallery noise, but it hides pinned actions while you are judging a screen.
