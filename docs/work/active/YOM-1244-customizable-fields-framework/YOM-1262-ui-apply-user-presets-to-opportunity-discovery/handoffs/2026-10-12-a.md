## What Was Done

The decision is in `feature.md`, under 2026-10-12. Paths are under
`src/web/src/components/Opportunity/TabbedDetails/`.

1. **`DetailFactBadges.tsx` is now a row of small chips:** an icon and the value in a white pill,
   34px tall, wrapping as needed. There are no tints, no expansion and no Escape handling. Pink
   is used only within 7 days of the deadline.
2. **`detailSectionJump.ts` (new):** a chip dispatches `yoma:detail-section-jump` with its section.
   `OpportunityDetailSections` listens, then opens the section, marks it, scrolls it under the
   sticky panel and focuses its button.
3. **`detailBadges.ts`:**
   - each badge carries its `section`;
   - `panel` is optional, and only How, Dates and Places have one;
   - the Pay and Reward panels, `spelledPay` and the `cashOutClosed` option are gone.
4. **`OpportunityDetailSections.tsx`:** new About sections "How you take part", "Dates" and
   "Places", rendered by `BadgePanel` (facts, the places bar, paragraphs). "Time needed" remains
   only when there is no How chip.
5. **`detailSectionCopy.ts`:** the "Pay and rewards" wording now matches the old panels.
6. **`DetailHeaderCard`** and **`OpportunityPublicDetails`** no longer pass `cashOutClosed` or
   `preview` to the header.

## Gotchas

- **The jump relies on DetailDisclosure's ids.** The card is the parent of
  `#section-{id}-body`. Renaming that id breaks the scroll silently: the section still opens,
  but the page doesn't move.
- **A chip's section must exist below.** Pay and Reward always have "Pay and rewards" (they only
  exist with an incentive). If `incentiveCopy` ever returns null while a Pay or Reward chip
  shows, the tap does nothing.
